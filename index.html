About the school

What we're actually like day to day

Hawthorn Ridge runs on the idea that students do their best work when someone actually knows their name. Average class size sits at 16, advisors stay with the same students for years, and the front office handles everything from bus routes to lost sweaters — usually within the same day.

640students, grades 6–12
16:1student–teacher ratio
63years serving Ridgefield

We're a single campus — one building, one gym, one library — which means questions rarely need to bounce between departments. If it's about your student, the front office can usually answer it directly, or route it to the right teacher, coach, or counselor the same day.

Families tell us the thing they notice most isn't the building. It's that someone calls back.

Academics

Three tracks, one building

College Preparatory

The core track for grades 6–12: four years of English, math through pre-calculus or calculus, lab sciences, and a world language sequence.

Honors & AP

Ten Advanced Placement courses across the sciences, humanities, and math, plus honors sections starting in grade 9.

Arts & Athletics

Band, choir, and studio art run alongside a full interscholastic sports program in soccer, basketball, track, and swimming.

Admissions

How to apply

  1. Submit the inquiry form

    Tell us your student's grade level and target start term. This opens their applicant file.

  2. Send transcripts and records

    Two years of report cards, plus any IEP or 504 documentation if applicable.

  3. Schedule a visit

    A half-day campus visit and a short, low-pressure conversation with an admissions counselor.

  4. Receive a decision

    Rolling admissions — most families hear back within three weeks of a completed file.
